# Ryom-Verzeichnis

The *Ryom-Verzeichnis* (RV) is the standard catalogue of the works of Antonio Vivaldi, compiled by the Danish musicologist Peter Ryom.

## About the Catalogue

The catalogue provides a systematic numbering of Vivaldi's works and is widely used to identify his compositions.

| Property | Information |
|---|---|
| Abbreviation | RV |
| Composer | Antonio Vivaldi |
| Compiler | Peter Ryom |
| Catalogue type | Work catalogue |
| First published | 1973 |

## Catalogue Sections

The RV catalogue contains works in several categories, including:

- Concertos
- Sonatas
- Sinfonias
- Chamber music
- Sacred vocal music
- Secular vocal music
- Operas
- Oratorios
- Other vocal and instrumental works
