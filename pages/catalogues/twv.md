# Telemann-Werke-Verzeichnis

The *Telemann-Werke-Verzeichnis* (TWV) is the catalogue of the works of Georg Philipp Telemann.

## About the Catalogue

The TWV catalogue provides a systematic numbering system for identifying the works of Georg Philipp Telemann. It was compiled by Werner Menke and first published between 1941 and 1951.

| Property | Information |
|---|---|
| Abbreviation | TWV |
| Composer | Georg Philipp Telemann |
| Compiler | Werner Menke |
| Catalogue type | Work catalogue |
| First published | 1941–1951 |

## Catalogue Sections

The TWV catalogue is organized into several major sections according to genre and type of composition.

| TWV Range | Category |
|---|---|
| TWV 1 – TWV 15 | Sacred vocal music |
| TWV 20 – TWV 25 | Secular vocal music |
| TWV 30 – TWV 39 | Music for keyboard instruments and lute |
| TWV 40 – TWV 45 | Chamber music |
| TWV 50 – TWV 55 | Orchestral music |

### Sacred Vocal Music

**TWV 1 – TWV 15**

Sacred vocal works, including church cantatas, oratorios, passions, and other liturgical and devotional compositions.

### Secular Vocal Music

**TWV 20 – TWV 25**

Secular vocal works, including operas, serenatas, odes, and other dramatic or occasional compositions.

### Keyboard and Lute Music

**TWV 30 – TWV 39**

Works for keyboard instruments and lute, including compositions for harpsichord, organ, and related instruments.

### Chamber Music

**TWV 40 – TWV 45**

Chamber music, including sonatas, trios, quartets, and other instrumental ensemble works.

### Orchestral Music

**TWV 50 – TWV 55**

Orchestral works, including overtures, suites, concertos, and other works for orchestra.

## Key Designations

In TWV numbers, uppercase and lowercase letters indicate the major or minor mode of the work.

| Designation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Uppercase | Major |
| Lowercase | Minor |

For example:

| TWV | Key |
|---|---|
| TWV 51:D1 | D major |
| TWV 51:d1 | D minor |
| TWV 52:A2 | A major |
| TWV 52:a2 | A minor |
| TWV 55:e3 | E minor |

The letter is followed by a number that distinguishes individual works within the same catalogue category and key.

A TWV number generally consists of several components:
