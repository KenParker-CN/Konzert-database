# Catalogues

A collection of major catalogues used to identify and classify musical works.

## Listings

### RV · Ryom-Verzeichnis

**Antonio Vivaldi**

The *Ryom-Verzeichnis* (RV) is the standard catalogue of works by Antonio Vivaldi, compiled by the Danish musicologist Peter Ryom.

[Browse RV catalogue](/pages/catalogues/rv)

### TWV · Telemann-Werke-Verzeichnis

**Georg Philipp Telemann**

The *Telemann-Werke-Verzeichnis* (TWV) is the catalogue system used to identify the works of Georg Philipp Telemann.

## About

Catalogue numbers provide a standardized way to identify individual musical works. Different composers use different catalogue systems, and a single work may sometimes be identified by more than one catalogue number.
