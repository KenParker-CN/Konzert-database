# Early Music

Early music encompasses the musical traditions of the Medieval, Renaissance, and Baroque periods, as well as the repertories and performance practices associated with them.

This section explores composers, works, instruments, recordings, and historical performance practices from the Middle Ages to the eighteenth century.
